A family-owned electrical contractor

Big Red Electric Company serves Las Vegas, Henderson and the surrounding Clark County area. Owner AJ leads the company and is a union-trained electrician with more than 10 years of experience. The team takes on residential and commercial electrical work, including Level 2 EV charger installation, panel and subpanel upgrades, wiring, outlets and switches, lighting, surge protection and commercial tenant improvements.

We keep the start of a project clear. Tell us what work you’re considering, and our team will review the job, explain the proposed work and provide an estimate before scheduling. You can call to discuss a specific service or ask whether your project is a fit.
